This policy explains how we handle personal information when communicating with customers through WhatsApp and operating our messaging application.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect and store:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages you send us, including replies to message buttons.
  • Messages and promotional images we send you.
  • Your messaging preferences and records of consent or withdrawal.
  • Contact-group memberships used to organize communications.
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, delivery statuses, and error information.
  • Technical information recorded by our hosting and connectivity providers, such as connection and request logs.

Contact details may be provided directly by you or entered by our authorized staff from our customer records. Adding someone to a contact group does not by itself establish consent to receive marketing.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer service.
  • Send relevant business communications.
  • Send promotional offers where we have the required permission.
  • Record and manage communication preferences.
  • Track message delivery and investigate technical problems.
  • Protect the application against misuse and meet legal obligations.

Marketing preferences

You can ask us to stop promotional messages by sending STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to our WhatsApp business number, or by emailing jjsmancherikalamoffice@gmail.com.

Where a message includes preference buttons, you may also use those buttons to communicate your choice. If your preference is not reflected, please contact us directly.

Withdrawing marketing consent does not necessarily require us to erase records that we need for legal purposes or to remember your opt-out.

Sharing and service providers

We use Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform to send and receive WhatsApp messages. Message content, phone numbers, media, and delivery information are processed through Meta’s services as needed to provide messaging.

We operate the messaging application and database on locally managed, on-premises servers. Connectivity providers may process information needed to deliver their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and prevent misuse.

We do not sell customer personal information.

WhatsApp’s own processing is explained in its privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Application records are stored on locally managed, on-premises servers.

We retain customer and message records for three years by default. We will handle customer deletion requests in accordance with applicable law. Records may be retained longer where required for legal obligations, dispute resolution, or maintaining opt-out preferences.

Backups may retain deleted information for up to seven years under our backup schedule.

Service providers may process information in other countries, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.

Security

We use access controls and other measures intended to protect information. Access to the messaging desk is restricted to authorized operators. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Cookies

The messaging desk uses a session cookie to keep authorized operators signed in. Customers do not need to sign in to the desk to communicate with us through WhatsApp.

Your requests and data deletion

Contact jjsmancherikalamoffice@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, or a change to your communication preferences.

For a deletion request, include “Data deletion request” in the subject and provide the WhatsApp number associated with your communications, including the country code. We may request reasonable information to verify that the request concerns your data. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and explain any information that must be retained. Deleting our records does not remove copies independently held by you, WhatsApp, or other recipients.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
